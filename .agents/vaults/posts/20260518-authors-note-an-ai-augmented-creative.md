BURNOUT is a purely creative project, with text, images and videos exclusively created by humans, for humans, in alignment with Blackaft’s principles, the biztech venture firm founded by George Kary. But the project has long been augmented by AI chatbots; at first, it was ChatGPT and then it became Gemini. That is, to say, augmentation strictly focused on research, context building, transposing data and, of course, refining iterations through a post-mortem process of analysing human-made content and metrics.

This is all just a fancy way to say humans make stuff by using AI before and after the humans-making-stuff process takes place.

Follow the experiment.

Balancing both sides of the aisle has been a massive hurdle, especially in terms of record keeping. You know how it goes: you open a conversation on ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, etc., only to have the damn thing locked in that particular session, essentially unavailable to all other chats.

Naturally tackling launches, pivots and AI adoption, Blackaft employed an AI-augmentation-first approach from the start.

It also meant that launches, pivots and especially AI adoption may and will require human creativity, which is essentially what this series gradually became across the many months that George Kary has been going at it.

A practice run.

Blackaft Studio.

BURNOUT now lives under https://github.com/blackaft, with a little bit of Google Drive and Google Photos for handling multimedia.

Screenshot of the Blackaft Studio repository on Github, which contains the firm’s creative projects, like the Burnout Series.

For the devs/biztech folk out there, the setup - and Blackaft’s philosophy - is generally agnostic in terms of the tool or model, as a core foundational premise to be able to serve clients by embedding into their own operations to more effectively tackle launches and pivots. Since the repository also adheres to the firm’s central infrastructure direction, the cost to initialise such a thing with a VS Code + Cline + BYOK via Google’s Agent Platform setup was a meagre $0.98.

Mind you, the agent was on auto-pilot. It just received a single command, and 3-4 additional prompts that were specific to the Burnout Series.

The process is also self-improving; meaning there are deterministic workflows inherent to the agentic setup focused on iterating minor improvements the more data the repository builds (i.e. knowledge bases or LLM Vaults).

Suffice to say, Gemini remains the king across benchmarking in terms of handling context for such type of processes in building up blocks on top of blocks.

Results.

Why does this entire thing matter? Sure, it sounds fancy; a creative project using such a “coding” level of AI?

Outside helping with the actual production content and schedule, this post was crafted by a human in less than 10 minutes. The thoughts and raw notes were already there since September 2025. The AI, operating inside that Blackaft Studio agentic setup, was able to provide the human with all the necessary context.

And also, every post now has a prompt. Made by machines, for machines. This is, after all, all about embracing the world’s impending duality of the extremes.

Machines on the back; humans at the front.
