BURNOUT is a series that intentionally embraces the complexity of multi-thread narration, both in the story and the meta. Both represent inexplicable pieces of the project’s own deconstruction-in-construction process, since the project itself is the actual meta-storyline.

But that wouldn’t really work without AI, these days, would it?

You’d immediately lose your audience.

Follow the experiment.

So, it was of paramount importance to embrace the fact that audiences absolutely use AI these days to dive and understand. However, using AI to research and analyse the material that’s published, in a silo, is just one side of the coin.

The other side is letting audiences tap into your project’s knowledge base.

So, we released a custom GPT to help audiences explore their thoughts on posts - both story and meta.

But, it’s got no spoilers or hints, don’t get too excited.

How it works.

Every time a new post springs up on Substack, the custom GPT’s knowledge base is extended with the new post - and any complementary, vibe-setting, photos or additional context awareness instructions and data.

You’ll find the link at the bottom of posts and on the menu.

Click it, and off you go.

You’ll need at least a free account with ChatGPT to use it.

How to make your own.

You’ll need a paid account with ChatGPT to create one.

Head over to chatgpt.com and make sure you’re signed in with your desired account at the bottom left.

Go to settings and navigate to:

Billing ~ verify your desired public name

Account ~ quickly setup your GPT builder profile, which allows you to optionally connect and publicly display your LinkedIn / GitHub profiles

Back to the left panel, (more), GPTs, and on the top right corner, hit “Create”.
