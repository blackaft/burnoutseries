BURNOUT’s posts on Substack now include a “listen to the post” feature with George Kary. This is in par with the project’s focus on up-skilling; the addition of audio offers the opportunity to practice audio mixing and American english. At some point, summary videos will also be introduced, to close the loop of embracing audiovisual formats.

Follow the experiment.

Subscribe now

The necessity for tackling audio design arose from a re-shuffle in the team - as we had to both recall quite a bit of material and reconsider the project’s production pipeline for its audiovisual components.

However, a side benefit of this extra challenge seems to be leading to an even higher quality in sound design, especially for the story’s mood setting videos.

So, please bare with us as we learn yet another discipline.

In terms of placement on the Substack posts, after a little bit of experimentation, we decided to place the audio snippets beneath the posts’s cover. The goal is to immerse readers with the visual parts first - that’s the cover and at a later stage, the summary video with George Kary - then, right before the drop cap initiating the text portion of the post, comes the audio snippet.

Readers can just click to listen, instead of, well, reading. Ah…

For the curious, the drop cap is the capitalised letter at the beginning of text. In our case, we intentionally always start posts with the word “BURNOUT”.

At least for the meta-story; the story’s chapters, of course, vary.

We’ll be updating posts from the past as we go along.

Finally, we also updated all the covers and cover templates for posts; the BURNOUT logo was taking too much real estate on the creatives.

After all, burnout is another one of the story’s misdirections.
