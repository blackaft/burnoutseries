BURNOUT is an experimental, multi-disciplinary project focused on upskilling, with 4 phases: setting the stage, the build-up, the release of the story and a retrospective BTS-focused part. We’re currently approaching the end of the first phase and entering the build-up stage, which means the focus will now start to shift to the multimedia aspect.

So, let’s take a look at the agenda.

Follow the experiment.

Subscribe now

Owing to the story’s own modular architecture relying on the three major acts of storytelling, the project’s phases, are, well, acts themselves; aiming to make audiences feel that distinct act-change effect on the project level, as well.

But more on that, later. Back to the phases.

Act 1: Setting the stage.

This phase was necessary for various reasons, however, the core premise was to turn a largely unorganised attempt that started in August of 2025 (a year ago) into a coherent ecosystem with a meta-narrative for creating the narrative itself.

Simply put, we had no idea what we were doing for months, despite the lingering presence of an architecture that had not taken shape, yet.
