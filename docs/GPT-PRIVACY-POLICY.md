# Privacy Policy

The Burnout Series GPT retrieves publicly available knowledge from the Burnout Series GitHub repository.

The GPT does not collect, store or process personal data through its custom actions.

Requests to GitHub's raw content endpoints are used solely to retrieve the latest published knowledge base.

The Burnout Series GPT retrieves publicly available knowledge files from this repository through read-only HTTP requests. The custom action does not collect or store personal information. Conversations with ChatGPT are governed by OpenAI's policies.
